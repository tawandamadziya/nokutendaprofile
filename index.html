About Nokutenda Amelia Tombindo

Nokutenda blends travel consulting with sustainability, cultural heritage, African music, and a genuine love for technology. Her approach is rooted in globetrotting with purpose: spending time with locals, learning from communities, and creating experiences that respect place, people, culture, and environment.
