About

Purposeful travel, culture, rhythm, and practical ideas.

Nokutenda, also known as Amelia, brings together travel consulting, sustainability, cultural heritage, African music, and an interest in technology. Her work is grounded in local connection, respectful curiosity, and useful planning.

She focuses on experiences that feel human and considered: spending time with communities, learning from place, and building plans that respect people, culture, and environment.

  • Practical sustainability support for projects and experiences
